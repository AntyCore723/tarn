# Legal Notice

## Disclaimer

Tarn is a **network research and privacy tool**. It is **not** a VPN service, proxy
service, or censorship circumvention tool. Tarn does not provide, operate, or
facilitate access to any network, service, or content.

## What Tarn Does

Tarn enables users to:
- Route browser traffic through a tunnel **they** configure
- Apply browser-level privacy protections (fingerprinting noise, header management)
- Use a local packet filter for network traffic analysis
- Configure DNS-over-HTTPS for system-wide DNS privacy

## What Tarn Does NOT Do

Tarn does not:
- Provide VPN servers or endpoints
- Sell subscriptions or access to any service
- Guarantee access to any specific website or service
- Operate as a proxy or relay for third-party traffic
- Circumvent access controls as defined by DMCA §1201. Tarn's optional DPI
  analysis feature operates on network traffic characteristics (packet
  timing, fragmentation) for research and diagnostics purposes; it is not
  designed to defeat access controls, DRM, or authentication measures
